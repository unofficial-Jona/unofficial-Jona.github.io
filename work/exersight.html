Venture / 2025—

Exersight.

A computer-vision system that turns activity in the free-weight area into automatic workout tracking and real-time feedback.

Role
Co-founder & CTO
Focus
Computer vision
Context
Strength training

The product

Workout data without interrupting the workout.

Exersight is designed for the part of the gym where conventional machine integrations do not reach. A camera observes the training area while computer-vision models identify athletes and exercises, count repetitions, and provide feedback.

As co-founder and CTO, I work across the technical architecture, computer-vision pipeline, and the product decisions needed to move from a model demonstration to a system that can operate in a real gym.
